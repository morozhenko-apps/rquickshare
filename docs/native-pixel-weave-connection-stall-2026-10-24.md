## 2026-10-24 VERIFIED SOURCE ROOT CAUSE: StartNotify callback blocked on whole worker lifetime

**Source proof from the exact pinned dependency version:** repository Core builds `bluer 0.17.3`. Its released [v0.17.3 `bluer/src/gatt/local.rs`](https://github.com/bluez/bluer/blob/v0.17.3/bluer/src/gatt/local.rs) handles the D-Bus `StartNotify` request with `notify_fn(notifier).await; Ok(())`. Our production `core_lib/src/hdl/gatt.rs` previously called `let started = tasks.spawn(...)` and then `started.await` **inside that callback**. Therefore D-Bus could not answer `StartNotify` until a potentially entire BLE/Weave transfer worker terminated. That is a concrete lifecycle bug consistent with first-send Android `Connecting…` being stuck until near the 60s Receive expiry, even though another transport route could complete. Not all previous failures are automatically proven to share this cause.

**Architectural patch on dev:** [`9bf88bd4`](https://github.com/morozhenko-apps/rquickshare/commit/9bf88bd46aabb8258010105e3f791c58a19559cb) changes the callback to submit the worker to the *existing* bounded `SessionTasks` and **return immediately after successful submission**, not await its `JoinHandle`. The `TaskTracker` still owns the worker to termination, and the worker keeps the one-permit BLE bridge lease; joining all tasks on `tasks.stop()` continues. Failed admission immediately drops the worker future and its lease, with an explicit warning; no orphan task, no time-based retry or peer-crossing shortcut. Production helper `start_managed_notify_worker` is exercised by two deterministic tests: early callback return with a live, occupied bridge; closed manager fails and releases permit. Add [`1644ccca`](https://github.com/morozhenko-apps/rquickshare/commit/1644cccadace09cabfb8d0993b87db8bf3879843) for a static `gatt_notify_callback_returning` trace marker, which documents callback readiness but **not independently D-Bus response delivery**.

Previous focused inbound evidence markers are kept for other candidate stalls, including `gatt_first_data_write_queued`, `gatt_first_data_packet_received`, `gatt_first_data_last_fragment`, `gatt_first_frame_forwarded`, `gatt_notify_requested/busy/worker_exited` from trace commits `e81fa9ed` and `6cac9a81`.
